# Localution procedure videos

These sixteen silent videos explain the preparation, expected output and next action for each task in `app/lib/tool-catalog.ts`.

They are **procedure motion graphics**, not screen recordings and not evidence of successful external collection, authentication, saving or publication. Account and external-tool requirements are shown in the videos. No customer records, tokens or account screens are included.

Each task has a twelve-second H.264 MP4, JPEG poster and Korean WebVTT captions. Korean text is also burned into the video. They use the self-hosted Pretendard Variable font distributed with its license under `/fonts/pretendard/`.

Rebuild with `python scripts/tutorial-video-render.py` from the repository root. This requires ffmpeg, Pillow and fontTools with WOFF2 support. No paid generation service is called.

The isometric geometric layers are illustrative motion graphics, not an interactive product interface. The surrounding player should provide explicit play and pause controls, a text alternative, no automatic playback and reduced-motion support.
